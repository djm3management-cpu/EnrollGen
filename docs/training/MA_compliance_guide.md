# Medicare Advantage Agent Compliance & Call Guide

*For Internal Agent Training Only | Updated April 2026*

*All agents must follow current CMS guidelines and carrier-specific compliance requirements.*

---

## 1. Call Opening

Every MA sales call starts the same way. Hit these three marks before anything else.

### 1.1 Identify Yourself

**RULE:** State your full name, that you are a licensed sales agent, and the agency you represent. CMS 42 CFR 422.2274(b).

**Say this:**
> "Hello, my name is [Agent Name], and I am a licensed sales agent with [Agency Name]."

### 1.2 Recording Consent

**RULE:** The call must be recorded in full. The beneficiary must explicitly agree. MMCM CH 2: 40.1.3.

**Say this:**
> "This call is being recorded for quality and compliance purposes. Do I have your permission to continue?"

**DO NOT:** Never assume consent. If they say no, you cannot proceed.

### 1.3 Verify Authority to Enroll

**RULE:** Only the beneficiary or their authorized legal representative can complete enrollment. MMCM CH 2: 40.2.1.

**Say this:**
> "Are you enrolling yourself today, or are you an authorized representative with legal documentation to act on behalf of the beneficiary?"

**DO NOT:** A spouse or family member cannot enroll someone without legal authorization. If they are not authorized, ask to speak with the beneficiary directly.

## 2. Required Disclosures

### 2.1 TPMO Disclaimer (First 60 Seconds)

**RULE:** Must be stated verbatim within the first minute of every sales call. 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(41).

**Say this:**
> "I do not offer every plan available in your area. Currently, I represent [number] organizations, which offer [number] plans in your area. You can always contact Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE, or your local State Health Insurance Program (SHIP) to get information on all of your options."

**DO NOT:** Never skip this or paraphrase it. This is verbatim. Every call.

### 2.2 No Misleading Information

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2262 and 422.2268 prohibit overstating benefits, implying plans are endorsed by Medicare or CMS, or pressuring enrollment.

**DO NOT:** Never say a plan is "the best," "endorsed by Medicare," or that they will "lose benefits" if they do not enroll today.

### 2.3 Pre-Enrollment Disclosures

Before completing any enrollment, read the following disclosures. These are carrier-specific but always include:

| Disclosure | What to Say |
|---|---|
| Plan contract status | [Carrier] is a Medicare Advantage organization with a Medicare contract. Enrollment depends on contract renewal. |
| Part A & B requirement | You must keep both Part A and Part B to stay in this plan. You must continue to pay your Part B premium. |
| Single MA plan rule | You can only be in one Medicare Advantage plan at a time. Enrolling will end your enrollment in any other Medicare health or drug plan. |
| Not Medigap | This plan is NOT Medicare Supplement insurance. |
| PDP restriction (MA-PD) | This plan includes drug coverage. You cannot enroll in a separate stand-alone Part D plan. |
| Annual changes | Benefits, premiums, and copayments may change on January 1 of each year. |
| Privacy | By joining this plan, your information will be shared with Medicare for enrollment tracking, payments, and purposes allowed by federal law. |

## 3. Scope of Appointment (SOA)

Since these are inbound member-initiated calls, the 48-hour advance rule does not apply. You still need a verbal SOA before discussing any plan details.

### 3.1 When to Obtain

**RULE:** Immediately after the opening, before presenting any plan information. CMS 42 CFR 422.2260-422.2274.

### 3.2 SOA Script

**Say this:**
> "Before we continue, I need to confirm the purpose of this call. Today we can discuss Medicare Advantage and/or Prescription Drug Plans. This authorization is required by CMS to ensure you receive only the information you request. By continuing, you acknowledge that this conversation does not obligate you to enroll, and that enrolling in a plan will change your Medicare coverage. Do I have your permission to proceed?"

**If they say yes:** Proceed with plan discussion.

**If they say no:**

**Say this:**
> "No problem. I can only discuss plans with your authorization. If you change your mind, you are welcome to call back anytime."

End the call. Do not push.

### 3.3 Mid-Call Plan Type Change

**RULE:** If the beneficiary asks about a plan type not in the original SOA, pause and get a new SOA before continuing.

**Say this:**
> "I would be happy to discuss that. Since it was not in our original scope, I need to confirm your permission. Would you like to proceed with discussing [new plan type] in addition to what we have already covered?"

### 3.4 SOA Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| Discussing plan details before getting the SOA | Violates CMS. If you realize it happened, stop and restart. |
| Paraphrasing or shortening the SOA language | Must be read as written. Do not improvise. |
| Discussing plans not covered by the SOA | Each plan type needs explicit consent. |
| Requiring an SOA for general Medicare questions | SOA is only for plan marketing, not education or billing help. |
| Pressuring consent | The SOA must be voluntary. Never imply they need to agree. |
| Adding the SOA retroactively | If you missed it, start over. You cannot backfill. |

## 4. Eligibility Verification

Before processing enrollment, confirm all four of these.

### 4.1 Medicare Entitlement

**RULE:** Beneficiary must have Part A and be enrolled in Part B. MMCM CH 2: 20.

**Say this:**
> "Before we proceed, I need to verify that you are entitled to Medicare Part A and enrolled in Part B. Do you have your Medicare card available to confirm this?"

### 4.2 Service Area

**RULE:** Beneficiary must live in the plan's service area at time of enrollment.

### 4.3 Election Period

**RULE:** You cannot accept an enrollment outside a valid election period. MMCM CH 2: 30.

| Election Period | When It Applies |
|---|---|
| ICEP (Initial Coverage) | First eligible for Part A & B |
| AEP (Annual Enrollment) | October 15 through December 7 |
| MA OEP | January 1 through March 31 (already in an MA plan) |
| SEP (Special Enrollment) | Qualifying life event (move, lost coverage, gained Medicaid, etc.) |

**Say this:**
> "To complete your enrollment, I need to confirm that you are within a valid election period. Are you new to Medicare, changing plans during AEP, or qualifying due to a special circumstance?"

### 4.4 Effective Date

**RULE:** The effective date depends on the election period. Confirm it with the beneficiary before enrollment. MMCM CH 2: 30.6.

| Election Period | Effective Date |
|---|---|
| ICEP | First of the month following enrollment |
| AEP | January 1 of the following year |
| MA OEP | First of the month following enrollment |
| SEP | Varies by circumstance |

**Say this:**
> "Based on your election period, your coverage will begin on [date]. Does that work for you?"

## 5. Summary of Benefits Review

You must walk the beneficiary through the plan's key features before enrollment. This is not optional.

### 5.1 Needs Assessment

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2264(c)(3)(ii)(D) allows reviewing the beneficiary's health needs, medications, and finances to find the right fit.

**Say this:**
> "To make sure we find the right plan, let me ask a few questions. Do you have any doctors or specialists you want to keep seeing? What prescriptions do you take regularly? Are you looking for dental, vision, or hearing coverage?"

### 5.2 Highly Utilized Benefits

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(5) requires clear explanation of commonly used benefits with cost-sharing details.

**Say this:**
> "Let me go over the most commonly used benefits. Primary care visits are [amount]. Specialist visits are [amount]. Hospital stays are [amount] per day for the first [number] days. For prescriptions, your copay depends on the drug tier. Want me to check your specific medications?"

### 5.3 PECL Review

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(4) requires reviewing the Plan Election Confirmation List during telephonic enrollments. This covers the EOC, provider network, pharmacy network, formulary, premiums, copays, coinsurance, emergency coverage, referral/prior auth rules, and how enrollment changes current coverage.

**DO NOT:** Never rush through this or skip it. If the beneficiary seems confused, slow down and explain again.

### 5.4 Confirm Understanding

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2274(c)(9)(i) requires confirming the beneficiary understands the plan before enrollment.

**Say this:**
> "Before we move forward, I want to confirm: you understand the plan's benefits and costs, you know how to find in-network providers, and you are aware that enrolling will replace your current Medicare coverage. Do you have any questions, or are you comfortable proceeding?"

## 6. Consent and Enrollment

### 6.1 State the Full Plan Name

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.2262(a)(1)(x) requires stating the full plan name including plan type (HMO, PPO, SNP). Never abbreviate.

**Say this:**
> "The plan you are enrolling in is [Carrier Name] [Plan Name] [Plan Type], which is a [HMO/PPO/SNP] Medicare Advantage plan."

**DO NOT:** Never say just "the Gold plan" or abbreviate the carrier or plan type.

### 6.2 Confirm Understanding

**Say this:**
> "Before we finalize: you understand the benefits and costs of this plan, including copays, deductibles, and the out-of-pocket maximum. You are aware this plan has a provider network. And you understand that enrolling will replace your current Medicare coverage. Do you understand and agree to proceed?"

### 6.3 Confirm Effective Date

**Say this:**
> "Based on your enrollment period, your coverage will begin on [date]. Does this effective date work for you?"

**DO NOT:** Never say coverage starts "immediately" or "right away." Always state the exact date.

### 6.4 Verbal Attestation

**RULE:** MMCM CH 2: 40.1.3 and 40.2 require a recorded verbal attestation of intent to enroll.

**Say this:**
> "We are now completing your enrollment request. To proceed, I need to confirm: you are voluntarily enrolling in [Carrier] [Plan Name] [Plan Type]. You understand that this plan will replace your current Medicare coverage. You agree to the effective date of [date]. Please state your full name and today's date to confirm your intent to enroll."

## 7. Voice Signature

The voice signature is the legal equivalent of a written signature for telephonic enrollments. Without it, the enrollment is not valid.

### 7.1 Before You Capture the Signature

Confirm these data elements are correct:

| Data Element | Source |
|---|---|
| Full name | Beneficiary states it |
| MBI or SSN | Beneficiary provides it |
| Plan name and type | Agent reads full name |
| Monthly premium | Agent states amount |
| Effective date | Based on election period |
| Election period | Confirmed in Section 4 |

### 7.2 Voice Signature Script

**Say this:**
> "To complete your enrollment, I need your official voice signature. By stating your full name and today's date, you confirm: you are voluntarily enrolling in [Carrier] [Plan Name] [Plan Type]; you understand this plan will replace your current Medicare coverage; and you acknowledge that this verbal signature is legally binding and serves as your official signature for this enrollment. Please state your full name and today's date."

### 7.3 Recording Requirements

**RULE:** The full enrollment call, including the voice signature, must be recorded and retained. CMS requires voice signatures to be accessible for audit. MMCM CH 2: Appendix 2.

**DO NOT:** A recording that only captures the signature but not the full enrollment process is non-compliant.

## 8. Call Closing

Before you end the call, hit every one of these.

### 8.1 Confirmation Number

**RULE:** MMCM CH 2: 40.1.3 and 40.4.1 require providing a tracking mechanism. Give them the confirmation number.

**Say this:**
> "Your enrollment has been submitted. Your confirmation number is [number]. Please write this down for your records."

### 8.2 Carrier Contact Info

**RULE:** 42 CFR 422.111(h)(1) requires providing the carrier's toll-free number and TTY service.

**Say this:**
> "If you need to contact [Carrier] for any reason, their customer service number is [toll-free number]. TTY users can call [TTY number]."

### 8.3 Next Steps

**RULE:** MMCM CH 2: 40.2 requires the beneficiary to know what happens after enrollment.

**Say this:**
> "You will receive a welcome packet from [Carrier] within 7 to 10 business days, which will include your new member ID card. If you have any questions before then, you can call their customer service line or contact me directly at [your number]. Thank you for your time today."

**DO NOT:** Never end with just "you're all set, bye." Always provide the confirmation number, carrier contact, and next steps.

## 9. Consumer Experience

Compliance is the floor. The call should also be a good experience for the beneficiary. This is how you build trust and close business without pressure.

### 9.1 Lead with Their Needs

Do not start by pitching a plan. Ask what matters to them first: doctors, prescriptions, budget, extra benefits. Then match a plan to what they told you.

**DO NOT:** Never open with "I have a great plan to show you." That is a pitch, not a consultation.

### 9.2 Present Value, Not Just Price

A $0 premium means nothing if the plan does not cover their doctors or drugs. Explain how the plan fits their specific situation. Cost is one factor among several.

### 9.3 Address Concerns Directly

If they hesitate, ask what is holding them back and answer it directly. Do not dismiss concerns or tell them not to worry. If you do not have the answer, say so and follow up.

### 9.4 Clear Call to Action

End every call with a next step. Either proceed to enrollment, or schedule a specific follow-up. Do not say "think about it and call back whenever."

**Say this:**
> "Based on what we discussed, would you like to move forward with enrollment today, or would you prefer I schedule a follow-up call so you can review the details first?"

### 9.5 Stay Focused

Phone calls have no visual cues. Listen carefully for confusion or disengagement. Speak clearly, do not rush, and check in regularly to make sure they are following along.

## Quick Reference: MA Call Flow

| Step | What You Do |
|---|---|
| 1. Open | Identify yourself, get recording consent, verify authority to enroll |
| 2. TPMO Disclaimer | Read verbatim within 60 seconds |
| 3. SOA | Get verbal scope of appointment before any plan discussion |
| 4. Eligibility | Verify Part A/B, service area, election period, effective date |
| 5. Needs Assessment | Ask about doctors, prescriptions, benefit priorities |
| 6. Benefits Review | Walk through SB, highly utilized benefits, PECL, cost-sharing |
| 7. Confirm Understanding | Beneficiary confirms they understand plan, network, costs, coverage change |
| 8. Pre-Enrollment Disclosures | Read carrier-specific disclaimers (contract, Part B premium, PDP restriction, etc.) |
| 9. Consent & Attestation | State full plan name/type, confirm effective date, get verbal attestation |
| 10. Voice Signature | Read signature script, beneficiary states name and date |
| 11. Close | Provide confirmation number, carrier toll-free/TTY, next steps |
