TPMO: all required elements within 90 seconds, before benefits, no first-minute rule and no SHIP. Organization fillers, digits/words, which/who/that offer, organizations/carriers/companies, plans/products, Medicare URL/phone variants and all-options/help-with-plan-choices wording are accepted. The observed ASR text "we do not offer free plan available in the area" is a narrowly accepted transcription variant when every other element is complete; this does not rewrite the spoken legal disclaimer.

SOA: named product categories and beneficiary agreement before a specific plan; no 48-hour wait. SOA date confirmation remains an agency item with original wording and points, not a reinstated waiting period.

Pre-enrollment topics: CMS topics need no exact checklist recital or extra yes. Evidence must be in plan presentation/enrollment before actual submission. Drug checks require coverage AND cost; current pharmacy/provider checks require network context. Intake questions alone fail. A submission object must explicitly be the application/enrollment, never an unrelated "submitted something". Enrollment section begins at an actual start/proceed announcement. Signature assent can follow name and DOB; the selected plan may appear anywhere in that section before submission. Unknown timing is REVIEW, not invented ordering.

Conduct: quoted findings only, AI REVIEW, no automatic deduction or AUTO-FAIL. Existing agency quality judgment can retain anchored stored pass/partial evidence with original weights; unsupported/negative AI findings remain REVIEW for admin decision. Unknown stored judgments are REVIEW with zero denominator rather than fabricated results.

Recording integrity/written TPMO consent need verified metadata; transcripts alone cannot prove them. Broad legacy cms_reference labels on agency items are not treated as verified speech requirements. Current combined 2027 enrollment guidance supersedes historical Chapter 2 references for 2027-effective enrollment; Part 423 parallels were checked for TPMO/scope/conduct, but this saved active template and cohort are MA, not a newly invented PDP rubric.
