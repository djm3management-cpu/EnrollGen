# CMS-only grading authority

Rules cy2027-cms-only-4: 21 active (14 code checks, 7 AI REVIEW rules); 141 excluded definitions. Every active item has a specific CMS basis. Agency scripts never authorize grading. The current saved-call cohort is MA only. This rubric is not applied to ACA, U65 or Med Supp; no blanket Part 422 assumptions are made for other products.

## Starting-list verification

- TPMO: §422.2267(e)(41)(i)-(ii) expressly requires the applicable complete disclaimer verbally before benefits. Counts/resources/timing are one rule, not separate penalties.
- SOA: §422.2264(c)(3)(i),(iii) requires recorded product agreement and limits marketing to agreed scope. No 48-hour waiting-period test.
- Pre-enrollment topics: §422.2274(c)(12) confirms the six requested topics and additionally names current pharmacy network participation. No exact recital or repeated yes is required for these topic checks.
- Coverage impact: the CMS enrollment mechanism and model acknowledgments require the effect on existing MA coverage, including relevant plan exceptions; the sales-script preference is not its authority.
- Enrollment consent: CMS enrollment guidance §40.1.4 requires recorded verbal intent to enroll; named-plan evidence is linked to that affirmative response.
- Confirmation number: tracking/receipt evidence is required, but a universal read-aloud number requirement was not established. No spoken-number deduction. This does not remove the plan tracking obligation.
- Federal contracting statement: §422.2267(e)(32) requires content on marketing materials. No explicit universal spoken live-sales-call obligation was established, so no grade was added.
- Conduct: seven narrowly scoped REVIEW rules have citations above. Pressure is an interpretive review under §422.2262 and the CMS-4212-F discussion at 91 FR 17453; incentive-driven steering cites §422.2274(c)(13) and may require contract facts. These are not automatic legal findings.

## Additional cited checks retained

Beyond the starting six topics, current pharmacy network discussion is explicit in §422.2274(c)(12). Recording consent for telephonic enrollment comes from guidance §40.1.4; entire-call retention and identified inter-TPMO written consent come from §422.2274(g). Marketing beyond agreed health-product scope is an additional REVIEW under §422.2264(c)(3)(iii). No agency greeting, travel, referral recital, recap, carrier-member-services phone or generic quality item is scored.

## Scoring mechanics

CMS does not prescribe this percentage/letter grade. Internal weights are one point per applicable deterministic requirement or administrator-confirmed violation. REVIEW and N/A are excluded; AI does not award compliance points or deduct them. This removes legacy agency weights and duplicate penalties. Only code with timestamped positive evidence of a late hard-rule event can AUTO-FAIL. Absence alone never invents an AUTO-FAIL quote.

Topic evidence is searched over the full sorted agent channel before the actual submission event, including during the application. Application-only questions do not qualify. When evidence exists but submission time is unknown, timing is REVIEW with zero points; no deadline is fabricated. The named plan and a clear beneficiary affirmative are required for enrollment consent. Original segment IDs and timestamped quotes are retained.
